The Last Mile

Then come the interviews.

Every round has a different goal. The person across the table is your user. Learn what each one needs to hear.

HR screen

"Are you real, and are we in the same range?"

Be clear and calm. Know your salary number before the call. No surprises.

Hiring manager

"Can you solve the problem I live with every day?"

Learn their pain before the call. Show them you have solved it before.

Team round

"Would we enjoy working with you?"

Ask real questions about their work. Curiosity beats a pitch.

Panel & case study

"How do you think under pressure?"

Think out loud. Structure first, then decide, then say why.

Leadership round

"Do you see the bigger picture?"

Talk outcomes and business, not tasks. Directors buy judgment.

The offer

"We would like to make you an offer."

Do not just say yes. The first number is a starting point, not a gift. This is where thousands are won.

You do not guess your way through this. On Nov 7 you build your Interviewer Profiler: paste in the job posting and who you are meeting, and it builds their persona and your prep sheet for that exact room. And when the offer comes, you will have the Offer Coach kit: it helps you read the package, set your anchor, and write the counter. I have sat on the hiring side of many offers. The first number is rarely the last.
