Build or revise the target company list at `memory/target-list.md`. This is the triangulation exercise: expertise x interests x market. Spray and pray is not a strategy.

Steps:

1. Read `memory/profile.md` first. If it has placeholders, stop and send them to /setup.
2. Interview, one question at a time: what are you actually good at (from the profile, confirm), what do you actually want to do, and then do real web research on where the market is hiring for that overlap right now. Real research, not vibes: search for companies hiring the target roles in their locations.
3. Propose 5 to 10 companies. For each, say why in one line. Let the user cut and add. A target list they do not believe in gets ignored by week two.
4. For every company that survives: find the careers page URL yourself, and write the role queries the way postings are written ("Product Manager", "Senior Product Manager", not "PM").
5. Write `memory/target-list.md` in the schema format: name, careers URL, role queries, why line. Every field filled. A company missing a field cannot be watched by /scout, so finish the job.

If the list already exists, this is a revision: read it, ask what changed (new interest, a company gone quiet, a rejection that taught something), and update rather than rebuild.

When done, one line: run /scout tomorrow morning and every morning after. That is the habit that makes this work.
