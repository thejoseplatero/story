The Warm Circle. Remember people properly. Never a sales pipeline: the job is a byproduct of the relationship, not the purpose of it.

State lives in `memory/warm-circle.md`. Read it first, always.

Route by intent from $ARGUMENTS:

- **A name and some context** ("add Sarah, met at the meetup, she runs design at a fintech"): add the person. Record who they are, where you met, what they care about, last touch (today), and a next-nudge date. Ask at most one follow-up question, only if the entry would be useless without it.
- **"who" / "review" / empty**: the circle review. List who is due or overdue a touch, one line each: name, days since last touch, the hook you have. If nobody is due, say so; silence kept honestly beats noise sent on schedule.
- **A name alone** ("Sarah"): pull up their entry and suggest the next move for that relationship.
- **"draft" plus a name**: draft the nudge.

Rules for any drafted message:

- Useful first. Lead with something for them: a link that fits what they care about, a genuine question about their work, a congratulations on something real. Check the entry for what they care about before drafting.
- If there is nothing genuine to say, the answer is "wait", not a hollow check-in. Say that plainly.
- No ask unless there is a real one, and never in a first touch after a long silence.
- The user's voice: simple English, short sentences, no em dashes, sounds like a person. Two or three sentences is usually right.
- Never send anything; the user sends. Draft, hand it over, log the touch only after they say it went out.

Every change writes back to `memory/warm-circle.md`, dated. Default nudge cadence: 6 to 8 weeks, sooner if their world moved (new role, launch, layoff news from /brief). When a morning brief mentions a company where someone in the circle works, that is a natural hook; point it out.

Most people only reach out when they need something. That is why it does not work. This kit exists so the user is not most people.
