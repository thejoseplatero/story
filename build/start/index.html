Build with Jose · the workshop

Three kits. Yours.

Plain text files. No code. Each one installs itself when you hand it to Claude, then runs as a conversation.

Before you start

  • A laptop. On your phone right now? Email yourself this link and do this tonight.
  • Claude Code, from claude.com/claude-code. It needs a paid Claude plan (from about $20 USD a month). It opens in a terminal, which looks scary for ten seconds and then is just chat.
  • Your resume as a file (PDF is fine), and 30 quiet minutes.

Install, once

1Make an empty folder, like my-job-search, and open Claude Code in it.
2Download a kit below, drag the file into Claude, and say: install this
3Claude installs it and tells you the one thing to try. That is everything.

The kits, in the order to run them

1 · Scout

Builds your target company list by interview, then watches it every morning.

DOWNLOAD
SAY
"build my target list"
IT ASKS
Four questions, one at a time: the role, where, what you care about, your edge.
YOU GET
10-12 proposed companies in three buckets. You cut to 8. Saved to a file it watches from then on: say "scout" any morning for news and new roles, or "tell me about [company]" for a five minute should-I-work-there read.
TIME
About 20 minutes first run. The morning check: 5 to 15 minutes, it runs while you make coffee.
WHAT A MORNING RUN LOOKS LIKE (SAMPLE)
NEW  Senior Product Designer at Wealthsimple, posted yesterday. Link in the brief.
Shopify: no design roles matching your query today.
KOHO: quiet week. Nothing worth your time. That is a valid finding.

2 · Audit

Reads your resume the way a hiring manager reads it. Ten seconds, then deep.

DOWNLOAD
SAY
/audit your-resume.pdf  (drag the file in, or paste the text)
IT ASKS
Only your target role, if you have not said it.
YOU GET
The honest ten second call (read or skip), then the three fixes that matter most, each with a rewrite built only from facts already on your page. Nothing invented.
TIME
About 5 minutes. Fixing what it finds: an evening.
WHAT THE OUTPUT LOOKS LIKE (SAMPLE)
The ten second read. A template sidebar, "passionate problem solver",
then your real title. My call: skip. Here is why, and the fix.

Fix 1. Your headline says adjectives. Say what you are: role, years, scope.
Fix 2. "Helped with the redesign" hides you. What did you actually do?
Fix 3. Your strongest work is on the last line of the page. Move it up.

3 · Pitch

When a real posting appears: a page that pitches you, in that company's own brand.

DOWNLOAD
SAY
"build my pitch page" plus the job posting link
IT ASKS
For your resume, the posting, and anything it will not guess. It scans the company's real site for their brand before writing a line.
YOU GET
A draft page in their look in about fifteen minutes: your experience mapped to their posting, one honest gap named on purpose. Works on your laptop immediately. When a role is real, say "full build" for the deep version with their brand measured properly and a link to send.
TIME
Draft: about 15 minutes. Full build: an hour-plus, worth it once per company you seriously want.

When something breaks

  • Paste the error into Claude and say: fix it. That is not a joke. That is how this whole way of working goes.
  • Files it creates live in a memory/ folder next to your kits, in plain text you can read and edit.
