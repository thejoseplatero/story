The problem

Two hundred applications. Two replies.

The worst part is not the no. It is the nothing. You check your email before you get out of bed, and there is nothing, again. Nobody teaches you the real rules of hiring here.

If that is your inbox, nothing is wrong with you. That is a process problem, and process problems have fixes.

The lesson

Five places people get stuck.

01You are applying to everything, and it is making every application worse.Spray and pray fails quietly. Aim at fewer companies, watched well.
02They looked you up. There was nothing there.Every serious application gets a search. Silence reads as a verdict.
03Your experience is not worth less here. It is just untranslated.The work is real. The page carrying it is not doing its job.
04Most people only reach out when they need something.That is exactly why it does not work. Be useful before you need anything.
05The interviewer is your user.Each round wants a different thing. Read the room before the room.
