What I bring, in Qatom's terms

Consumer scale on a points economy

I led digital product for Aeroplan, a loyalty currency millions of Canadians earn, spend, and settle every day. The closest consumer analogue to an agent wallet that exists.

Zero to one, inside constraints

Built product, design, and marketing technology orgs from scratch at Air Canada, shipping under regulation, legacy rails, and real money movement.

MCP-native, daily

I build with Claude Code and MCP servers every week: agents that research, publish, and file. I am the operator persona in your docs, not a tourist to it.

Teaching the market you need

I teach AI at BrainStation and run workshops for the builders who will operate these agents. Your adoption curve runs through rooms I am already in.

The honest gap

I have never shipped settlement infrastructure. Bearer assets, in-flight settlement, TODA underneath: that layer I would be learning from your engineers, not teaching them.

What I do bring to that layer: a decade of making regulated money products legible to normal people, which is the half of agentic commerce nobody has built yet.

What I would do in the first ninety days

01
Talk to twenty agent operators and twenty API earners. Find where the pain is strongest, not where the protocol is cleverest.
02
Define the one metric preview should optimize before pricing exists, and instrument it.
03
Ship the operator onboarding that makes the first paid tool call feel like nothing. Zero-fee means nothing if setup costs an afternoon.
